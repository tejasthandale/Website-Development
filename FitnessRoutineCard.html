Start the day with simple stretches for your arms, legs, and back.

Cardio Workout

Do sprints or jogs to increase energy and stamina.

Time: 30 minutes

Strength Training

Lift till failure at least twice.

Time: 1 Hour

Rest

Drink enough water and take proper rest after completing a workout.

2-3 Days

Fitness Tip: A good routine includes exercise with at most 2 minutes of rest
